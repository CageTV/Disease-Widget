Disease Widget (SKSE) - movable

The widget, its icons, definitions and source are by kuroishitam (https://www.nexusmods.com/skyrimspecialedition/mods/195393), whose mod page grants open permissions. This repository starts from the unmodified v1.1 source (first commit) and adds, in the second commit, mouse dragging of the widget (src/Hud.cpp, src/MCP.cpp, include/Hud.h) and a build path for non-VR runtimes (src/Manager.cpp).
